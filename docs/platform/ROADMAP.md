## 1. Roadmap Rules

- Accepted IMP identity is **permanently immutable**. Do not reinterpret or renumber accepted
  history (IMP-001 → IMP-025 and IMP-005A).
- No other document may independently redefine IMP numbering.
- Formal ROADMAP ledger IMP identifiers use `IMP-\d+[A-Z]?`.
- Only one product slice is normally active.
- A deferred capability cannot be assigned or promoted by an implementation agent.
- Roadmap changes require a `roadmapVersion` change.
- Coding-agent completion is not acceptance. Acceptance is recorded in [`STATE.md`](./STATE.md).
- The historical IMP-026 → IMP-028 controlled-continuation exception is **CLOSED**.
- **GTM-R138** records `CONTINUATION_EXCEPTION: IMP037_PROVIDER_BLOCKED_TO_IMP038`
  (authority PR#179/5771367844). It does not accept IMP-037 or activate IMP-039.
- **D-377** (`PROGRAM_PAUSE: PRE_GTM_PRODUCT_INSERTION_PROVIDER_BLOCKED`) remains CURRENT.
- **D-382** inserts IMP-036J before held IMP-037. Deals and Campaigns remain parked discovery.
- **D-383** allocates IMP-036K — Revenue Recommendations as `nextProductSlice` and amends
  D-382 only for that identity, activation, and sequencing.
